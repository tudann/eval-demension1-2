Claymation, stop-motion style: visible thumbprints in the clay, slightly jittery frame.
This video is a montage of four separate shots, with a hard cut between each shot.
Shot 1: a clay handyman presses a red plunger onto a kitchen floor and pulls it off with one hard yank. Cut.
Shot 2, new location: he presses the same red plunger onto a tiled wall and yanks it off. Two tiles crack and come loose. Cut.
Shot 3, new location: he presses the plunger onto a ceiling and hangs from the handle with both hands, feet off the floor. Cut.
Shot 4: still hanging, he gives one last hard pull. A whole ceiling panel tears off and falls down with him in a puff of clay dust.
