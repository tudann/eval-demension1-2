Aerial travel-reel footage, late golden light, long shadows across a sandstone plateau. One continuous take, with the camera flying alongside at the height of the plateau rim, keeping both paragliders in frame the whole time.
A red paraglider flies behind a yellow paraglider, low along the edge of the plateau.
The red paraglider climbs and closes the gap on the yellow one.
The red paraglider draws level with the yellow one. At that moment the video briefly goes into slow motion, then snaps back to normal speed.
The red paraglider pulls clearly ahead of the yellow one as both turn out over the cliff drop.
