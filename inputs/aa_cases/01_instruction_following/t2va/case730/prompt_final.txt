A title sequence in a screen-printed poster style, using only two colours: acid green and off-black. A grainy halftone dot texture shifts slightly from frame to frame.
This video has four cards. Each card has a locked-off camera; the three transitions between the cards carry all the movement. There is never a dissolve.
Card 1: a mad scientist wearing goggles, with wild white hair, raises a bubbling flask. Beside him, large stamped letters read THE VOLT WARD.
Transition 1: green liquid pours out of the flask toward the camera and covers the whole frame, acting as a foreground wipe.
Card 2: the wipe clears to show a tall coil throwing an arc of electricity.
Transition 2: a flash-to-white fires exactly at the peak of the arc.
Card 3: the white fades down onto the same scientist, grinning wildly and pulling a huge lever downward.
Transition 3: as the lever slams down, a match cut carries that same downward motion into the final card.
Card 4: the words THE VOLT WARD drop into place letter by letter and hold on screen.
