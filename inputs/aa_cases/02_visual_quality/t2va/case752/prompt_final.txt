Photoreal, overcast light, spray drifting from a wide waterfall. A low handheld camera follows alongside a hiker in an orange rain jacket at the base of the waterfall.
Five wet stepping stones cross the water in front of her. A flat-design teal card is pinned to each stone.
She leaps from stone to stone. Each time her boot lands on a stone, the card on that stone lights up with a soft chime over the roar of the waterfall.
On the fourth stone she wobbles. Her arms fly out wide and her weight rocks from heel to toe.
She steadies herself. Then she leaps to the fifth stone and lands on it, balanced. The app's logo card flips up in the air beside her.
