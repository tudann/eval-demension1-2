Photorealistic handheld event footage in bright midday sun, one clean continuous take, on a closed city street during a crowded charity fun run. The mood is joyful and loud.
The camera swings right across a group of joggers in tutus and capes, with race numbers pinned to their chests, their legs moving in steady running strides. The camera stops on a runner in a bulky foam hot-dog costume sprinting ahead of the group.
The camera holds steady on her. She plants both hands on the road in front of her. Her legs swing up and over her head together, and both feet land on the road at the same time, so she now faces backward. Then she jumps straight up and flips backward in the air under the inflatable finish arch. It is one full rotation in the air, knees tucked, legs held together, hands never touching the ground. She lands on both feet with knees bent, and her feet do not slide. She throws both arms up.
Confetti bursts and drifts close across the lens while the other runners keep jogging past behind her.
The take settles into a held end card in chunky rounded lettering: 'RUN SILLY. TRAIN SERIOUS. KINETIQ SHOES.', crisp and not warped.
