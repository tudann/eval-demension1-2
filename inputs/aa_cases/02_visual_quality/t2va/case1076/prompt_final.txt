[Style] Photorealistic wedding-film cinematography, romantic and airy, shallow depth of field. The video starts inside a dark stone chapel lit only by candles and ends outside in very bright, sun-washed daylight.
[Visual] Inside a dim stone chapel at noon. The only light comes from candle flames along the walls. A bride in a long white gown and a groom in a pale grey suit run hand in hand up the aisle toward tall open doors. The camera follows them from behind. Through the open doorway there is only pure white light: the doorway is so bright that nothing outside can be seen, just a white shape against the dark room. The camera follows the couple in one unbroken move up the aisle and out through the doors. As they step through the doorway, the whole frame washes to white. Then the brightness settles smoothly, one time only, into bright noon daylight. Now rows of guests are visible standing on the sunlit steps, throwing handfuls of pale flower petals over the couple. The last second holds on the petals drifting down and settling around the couple in the bright light.
[Avoid] No cut when they pass through the doorway. The inside of the chapel is never evenly lit. While the camera is still inside, nothing outside is ever visible through the doorway, only white. Once the daylight settles, the image never drifts back toward darkness.
