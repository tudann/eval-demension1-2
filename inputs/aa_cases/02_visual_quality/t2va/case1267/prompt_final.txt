[Style] Photorealistic coastal real estate film shot as a low, drifting drone aerial. Heavy overcast sky, silvery flat light over the sea, muted greys and warm cedar-wood tones.

[Subject] At low tide, an estate agent in rolled-up white linen trousers and a buyer in her forties walk barefoot across a wet, rippled sandbar. The buyer carries her sandals in one hand. They walk toward a wooden cedar house standing on tall timber stilts, fifty metres ahead of them. Every step presses a clear footprint into the dark wet sand behind them. Halfway there, a driftwood log lies across a shallow channel of tidal water. The buyer crosses the channel by walking along the log, arms spread wide. She tips once to the left. She shifts her weight onto the foot planted on the log and steadies herself. She steps off the log onto firm sand, where the agent waits and points up at the house.

[Visual] The camera glides low beside the two walkers at hip height. For the log crossing, it sinks to knee height near the sand. Then it angles up to show the stilt house behind them. The final second holds on the trail of footprints leading toward the house.

[Sounds] Gulls, small waves lapping through the channel, the soft wet suction sound of sand under each bare footstep.

[Avoid] No footprints appear ahead of a foot or fade away afterward. No feet slide across the sand. No other people on the sandbar. No subtitles.
