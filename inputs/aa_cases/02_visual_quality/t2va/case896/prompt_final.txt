[Style] Very clean tabletop commercial look, bright soft daylight from a big window, rich saturated colour, close visible texture on the dimpled lemon peel, shiny glazed ceramic and clear glass.
[Visual] The shot opens close on a large ceramic platter painted with fine blue-and-white stripes in rings, one ring inside the other. A bright lemon drops in from just above the frame. It bounces once on the glazed platter and rolls across the striped rings. Then the camera arcs slowly and smoothly about a quarter circle around the table in one continuous take. During this camera move, a hand cuts the lemon in half with a knife. The hand then squeezes one lemon half hard over a clear glass pitcher of water. Juice streams down in thin threads and spreads into the water in slow swirls. As the camera arcs, a soft window highlight sweeps across the glazed platter. The striped platter and the table behind the pitcher look bent and distorted through the glass, the way real glass bends whatever is behind it. The shot ends with the pitcher and the striped platter together in frame.
[Constraints] One single continuous take: the drop, then the cut, then the squeeze, in one flow. The painted stripes stay evenly spaced and fixed to the glazed surface while the lemon rolls and through the whole camera move. The lemon peel dents slightly under the squeezing fingers.
[Avoid] The stripes must not drift, warp, smear, or change in number. No extra lemons appear. Juice never passes through the wall of the pitcher. The stripe pattern never appears on the lemon or the cloth. The pitcher must look like clear glass, not dull plastic.
